Bottom Line

Over the last 14 days, the account ran at a 21.0x ROAS. Video ads and summer "drop" style creative are the biggest drivers. Video alone is running at roughly double the ROAS of photo ads, even though it is a small piece of the budget.

Our brand story ads (celebrating 10 years) are still profitable, just not built to sell directly. They are doing their job on awareness, and we are now tracking them that way instead of against sales targets.

Top Insights
Video and seasonal drops are carrying the account
Brand storytelling is still profitable, just not built to convert like a drop
Video works best, and we are not running enough of it
Our 5 video ads spent $3,519 and returned $121,164 in purchase value. Our 38 photo ads spent $11,132 and returned $186,272. Video is running at roughly double the ROAS of photos, on less than a quarter of the budget.
Video: 34.4x ROAS
Summer "drop" ads are driving almost all of our best results
Nearly every one of our top 6 ads is a "Summer Essentials" or "Hot Girl Summer" style ad, posting 17x to 47x ROAS. Our anniversary brand ads are still profitable, just noticeably softer.
Drop ads: up to 47x ROAS
Brand storytelling ads are building awareness, not driving sales
Our 10-year anniversary ads are converting well below the drop-style ads. That is expected. Going forward we will judge these against reach and brand awareness instead of holding them to a sales benchmark they were not built to hit.
Anniversary ads: 6.9x to 14x ROAS

Strategy Next Steps
Where we're taking creative into the next two weeks
Priorities coming out of our biweekly account sync
1
Make more video
Video is our best-performing format and our smallest budget line. We're producing more video built to run long term, using the same seasonal-drop style that's already converting.
2
Carry the drop style into new photo ads
New photo ads will use the same "Summer Essentials" and "Hot Girl Summer" style driving our top results, not the softer-performing anniversary style.
3
Move Essentials into fall without a reshoot
We're adapting the winning Summer Essentials footage with fall-appropriate messaging and a neutrals version, pulling in proof points from the evergreen assets like "100 washes later, no pilling." We're also working in Kirby's lifestyle imagery from the anniversary event to keep the evergreen library fresh without a new shoot.
4
Judge brand storytelling by awareness, not sales
We'll keep the anniversary ads running for brand building, but measure them against reach and awareness goals instead of holding them to the same sales bar as drop ads.
5
Start lining up holiday creative
Kirby is already planning shots two months out for Black Friday. We're syncing with her on shot lists now so holiday assets are ready well ahead of the season.
