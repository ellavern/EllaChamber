Creative Analysis

What's Working, and Why

A few patterns hold across the whole window.

🎯
Product-central statics with a direct headline win
The #1 creative by revenue, HGS Static 4, and the #3, DB Summer 3's original, both keep the product and the model centered against a plain background, paired with one short, direct line naming the moment. CTR on these sits at 1.26 to 1.41%, the best in the account.
HGS Static 4 CTR: 1.41%, best in the top 5
🎬
The winning video hook is a repeatable template
Vid 1, Vid 3, and Vid 5 all open the same way: a woman mid-stretch outdoors in the first few seconds, with fast text stacking the collection name on screen. Vid 3 turned that structure into the best return in the account (36.8x) on the smallest budget of any strong performer, a sign it's under-funded relative to what it's proving, not maxed out.
Vid 3: 36.8x return on just $128 spend
🖼️
Static-only concepts still carry real weight
Three of the top five creatives by revenue are static images, not video, so the account isn't video-dependent to hit strong returns. That matters directly for the evergreen static push: the format isn't the ceiling, the framing and copy are.
3 of the top 5 by revenue are statics
Next Two Weeks

What We're Making Next

Built directly off the working patterns above, pointed at the longevity story: doesn't pill, holds its shape wash after wash.

1
Build the product-central longevity statics
Match the framing that already won, HGS Static 4 and the DB Summer 3 original: product and model centered, clean background, image doing the visual work. Headline space is reserved for one direct, provable claim, "no pilling," "holds up after 50+ washes," instead of the abstract benefit lines that underperformed this window.
2
Consolidate the DB Summer 3 duplicate into the original
Same creative, one ad running at 34.8x and one at 15.7x. Moving the duplicate's budget into the original is a direct, no-new-creative lift, and it's already lined up on this week's plan.
3
Edit 3 to 4 new cuts on the Vid 1 / 3 / 5 template
Footage is already shot; the team is cutting new versions now. Same movement-forward open, fast text reveal, re-pointed at the longevity message instead of "new collection," the same pacing that's carrying the current top three videos.
4
Swap in the new longevity static as a head-to-head test
We'll run it directly against HGS Static 6 to see whether the provable-claim headline outperforms the more aspirational copy it's replacing. This is a hook-structure iteration, not a new concept, so it can go into testing fast.
Competitive Landscape

What Competitors Are Running Right Now

A look at what's currently active from two brands on the watchlist. This is a snapshot of live creative in market, not performance data, we don't have visibility into competitor spend or ROAS, only what's running.

Gymshark
Direct Competitor
Format strategy: movement-first, video-heavy. Four of the six most-visible active creatives are video, most of them product-in-motion demos rather than static shots, leaning harder into movement and proof-of-performance than our current static-heavy mix.

What this means for BuffBunny: video is king, and our data backs that up, but our top-five results show statics can carry just as much weight. We'll keep leaning on both, using video where it's already winning and letting the strongest statics keep earning their spot.
