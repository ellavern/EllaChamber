Key Insights

2 Things Worth Knowing

Both patterns show up across multiple ads, not a single standout.

01
Video beats static, even in the same campaigns
In our two proven Summer campaigns, the 6 video ads returned 36.6x ROAS at a $3.88 CPA. The 16 statics in those same campaigns returned 30.3x at $5.40. Video did it on only 17.5% of spend. We are re-editing the Summer Essentials videos for Fall, and raw footage from your team would let us test even more (see Creative Needs).

02
The proven Summer templates are doing the heavy lifting
The 22 ads in Summer Essentials and Hot Girl Summer took 51.7% of spend and returned 32.5x. The 19 ads in Metamorphosis and Cold Evergreen took 48.3% and returned 7.1x. We are putting the winning template into Fall colorways. Metamorphosis was a one time drop, so we are not building more of it.

How This Shapes Our Strategy

Formats: Oner and Vuori lean into video for new launches, while Fabletics tests one message across many statics. Hooks: Oner opens with first person creator lines and decision questions ("Mid rise vs. high rise"). Vuori leads with a benefit line ("Soft doesn't always mean still"). Promos: Vuori mostly runs full price, with one ad offering 20% off a first order. Oner leans on creator codes. Fabletics uses limited time intro offers. Fall themes: layering and new Fall colors (Vuori), cold weather (Fabletics), training season (Oner) and holiday onesies (Fabletics).

What we can borrow

  • Creator fit check videos with a code. Real lifters showing a rep day fit in their own words.
  • Decision hooks. "Which BuffBunny set is your leg day set?" fits our video results (Insight 01).
  • Fall arrival lines. A "new Fall color" style drop, said our way: strong first, cozy second.
  • A cold gym angle. Layers that still let you move, in BuffBunny's voice, not a copy of anyone's line.
  • One message, many versions. Take our best seasonal angle (Insight 02) and ship 6 or more variants in one batch.

Creative Needs

What We Would Love From Your Team

These are shots your team can get on a phone, during a shoot, or have affiliate creators make to provide more data driven assets for ads.

Video Your Team Can Create

Raw is fine. Real people in real BuffBunny gear is what works.

Creator fit check videos
A lifter or ambassador showing a rep day fit in their own words. 13 of Oner Active's 30 newest ads are creator videos with a promo code in the caption.
Two sets, one decision
Hold two fits side by side and say which one you would wear for which workout. Oner's "Mid rise vs. high rise" video is the model.
Cold weather layering try ons
Put on the layers, then squat, stretch and move. Vuori's Fall launches and Fabletics' "Cold temps, bold looks" push both lean on this theme.
Real workout footage in the gear
Squats, a run or a class, any camera. Our video ads returned 36.6x ROAS this window, so more raw footage means more tests.
Images We Need for Static Ads

Higher resolution is better. Plain backgrounds let us build on the template that is winning (one product, one direct headline).

Fall colorways on clean backgrounds
Product front and center, each new Fall color. Vuori's "New Fall Color: Bloom" and Oner's "New: Energy Orange" are color drop statics.
Cold weather lifestyle shots
Sets worn with layers, outdoors or at the gym. Fabletics is running one cold weather message across many static versions.
Fit and detail close ups
Back, side and front views plus fabric and seam details, so we can write fit and support headlines. Oner runs "Your glutes will thank you" as a static.
Customer and creator stills
Real people in BuffBunny sets, selfie style is fine. Oner mixes creator content in with product statics.
Holiday and gifting shots
Sets styled for the holidays or as gifts. Fabletics has a holiday onesie push running with a limited time intro offer.
