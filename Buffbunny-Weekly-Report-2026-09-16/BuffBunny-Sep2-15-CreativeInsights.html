Creative Analysis

What's Working, and Why

The winners have one thing in common: warm audience, not a specific format.

🎯
Warm audiences are carrying the account
Essentials Launches returned 14.36x on $6,761. Hot Girl Summer returned 12.16x on $3,806. Both are retargeting people who already know the brand. Together they drove 92.4% of the purchase value on 70.2% of the spend.
Combined warm campaign return: 13.57x
🎬
Format isn't the win, once you control for audience
Inside warm campaigns only, statics returned 13.39x and video returned 13.91x. Basically a tie. The account is winning because the message lands with people who already know BuffBunny, not because of a specific format.
Static 13.39x, video 13.91x, warm campaigns only
💰
HGS Static 13 could handle more budget
16.16x over the full 14 days on just $448 in spend. This week alone it also had the strongest click-earned return of any ad above $150. Two separate reads say the same thing: give it more room.
16.16x over 14 days, 11.21x this week
Creative Analysis

What's Not Working, and Why

Cold prospecting has had two full weeks to prove itself. It answered the question.

🛑
Cold prospecting isn't landing with people who don't know us yet
Every ad in the Evergreen campaign sits at the bottom of the account. It took 29.8% of spend for 7.6% of the purchase value, blending to 2.59x against 13.57x on warm campaigns. Not a one week dip. Braden reached the same conclusion in his own weekly report, working from a different window.
Cold 2.59x on $4,548, warm 13.57x on $10,567
💸
Our biggest ad spend is also one of our weakest
"EG | Cold | Move Freely No Restrictions" spent $1,966, more than any other ad in the window, and returned 2.01x. It's the clearest place to pull budget from right now.
$1,966 spend, 2.01x return, 25 purchases
⌛
A few of these have already run their course
Two of the six lowest performers are already paused in the account, and a third had zero orders this week per Braden's report. The budget left in cold is mostly sitting on ads that already told us what they had to tell us.
3 of 6 lowest performers are stopped or stopping
So What

What This Means

Braden's weekly numbers and this 14 day creative pull land on the same read. That's what makes the next move confident, not reactive.

Warm audience creative is working, and it isn't picky about format. Cold prospecting is not working, and it's not a format problem either. It's a message problem. So the plan is simple: keep feeding the proven warm templates, pull the budget cold is wasting, and use it to fund a real fix on the cold side instead of just spending less on the same ads.

Production Plan

Creative Needs

What we're building next on our end, and the raw content from you that would let us build even more.

What We're Making

Built off the templates that are already proven, plus a real fix for cold instead of just a smaller budget.

1
New statics on the DB Summer 3 / HGS Static 4 template
Product front and center, plain background, one direct claim in the headline. Same structure that's driving the top of the account. We just need to point it at the fall colors and new SKUs.
2
Motion graphics cuts of the top claim lines
Vid 1 and Vid 3 are winning on fast text stacking over movement. We can build cheaper motion graphics versions of that same structure, animated text over product stills, so we're not waiting on a full shoot to test new claims.
3
A real fix for cold, not just a smaller budget
New cold statics and a motion graphics cut testing a provable claim headline, something like "no pilling after 50 washes," instead of the aspirational lines that are underperforming now. This is the actual test, budget cuts alone don't fix the message.
4
Placement-specific cuts for Reels overlay and Audience Network
Both are running the same creative as everywhere else right now, but they're the cheapest, highest-return slots in the account this week. Worth a version sized and cropped for those placements specifically before we put real budget behind them.
Ads We'd Love From You

Real, natural footage from your side turns into some of our best-performing ads. The more of this you can send over, the more we can put into testing.

Get ready with me videos in BuffBunny sets
A quick GRWM clip, throwing on a set and heading out, is a natural hook and a natural outfit reveal in one. This format is doing well across the category and we don't have any of our own to test yet.
Gym and workout footage actually wearing the gear
Squats, a run, a class, doesn't need to be produced or even shot on a real camera. This is the raw material behind the video template that's already winning, so more of it means more tests without waiting on a shoot.
Styling or try-on style clips
Someone showing how they'd wear a set, mixing pieces, styling it for the gym versus running errands. Good source for new statics and a different angle than the straight product shot.
Real customer footage or reviews on durability
The claim angle we want to test on cold, "no pilling," "holds up wash after wash," lands harder with a real customer saying it on camera. Any UGC, reviews, or testimonials backing that up go straight into the new batch.
Fresh product shots for the fall line
We're stretching the Summer Essentials assets into September. New photography of the fall colors and new SKUs lets us build the next static batch on the winning template right away instead of reusing summer shots.
Sign off on how far we can push the claim language
Before we put money behind a "no pilling after 50 washes" style headline on cold, we want your green light on the claim itself so it doesn't stall once it's already in market.
